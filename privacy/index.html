WHAT THIS SITE MEASURES

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand which sections visitors actually read. Analytics is a non-essential measurement under CNIL guidance, so the Google tag is not present in the page and downloads nothing until you press Accept analytics.

Declining sets the same choice. Either way your answer is remembered on this device so the question is asked once, not on every page view.

What is measured

Page views, referrer, and which links get clicked — for example whether the CV button or a project link is used. This is aggregate traffic data used to decide what to keep on the site.

What is not

  • No advertising or ad-personalisation cookies.
  • No cross-site tracking, no remarketing audiences.
  • No data sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers.
  • No advertising ID, no fingerprinting.

Who receives it

Google Ireland Limited, as Google's EU establishment. Aggregate data may be processed in the United States under Google's standard contractual clauses.

You can opt out of Google Analytics for the whole web attools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what data is held about you, correct it, or have it erased. Write tocontact@h4md1.fr and it will be answered.
